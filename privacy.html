Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Easy processes and how it is used when you access the Link Prospect Checker.

1. Information we process

Depending on how you use Easy, we may process account information, authentication data handled by our authentication provider, subscription status, usage information, and data you choose to enter or upload, such as project settings, prospect databases, domain lists, and supported analysis files.

2. Temporary use without an account

Some limited functionality can be used without creating an account. Data used in this mode may remain only in your browser and is not intended to provide a persistent cloud workspace.

3. How we use information

Information is used to provide and maintain Easy, authenticate users, save eligible workspaces, apply plan and usage limits, process subscriptions, troubleshoot issues, prevent abuse, and provide support.

4. Payments

Subscription payments are processed by Paddle. Easy does not store your full payment card details. Paddle may process payment, billing, tax, and transaction information as required to act as Merchant of Record.

5. Service providers

Easy uses third-party infrastructure and services, including Supabase for backend and authentication functionality and Paddle for subscription payments.

6. Data retention and security

We retain account and workspace information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect Easy. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information where applicable. Certain records may need to be retained when required by law or legitimate operational obligations.
